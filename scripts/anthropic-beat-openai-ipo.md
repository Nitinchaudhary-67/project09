## Beat 1 — What actually happened

**VO:** Let's start with the facts, because the headlines are getting this slightly
wrong. On June first, Anthropic confidentially submitted a draft Form S-1 to the SEC.
In plain English: it filed the paperwork to go public. That's the first real step
toward an IPO. But notice the word "confidential." Anthropic is not publicly traded
yet. There's no ticker. No share price. The number of shares and the offer price
aren't set. All of that depends on SEC review and the market. So the right way to say
it: Anthropic has *filed* to go public. The IPO itself is proposed — not done.

**VO:** Now, the number everyone's screenshotting. Nine hundred sixty-five billion
