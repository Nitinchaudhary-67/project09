## Accuracy guardrails

- It is a **confidential / draft** filing — do NOT say Anthropic "is now publicly traded" or "IPO'd." It filed paperwork; the IPO is proposed.
- The $965B is a **private valuation** from the funding round, not a set IPO price.
- Revenue/run-rate figures are **company guidance to investors**, not audited.
- Verify OpenAI's exact IPO date as "reported/targeted," not confirmed.
