## Music / pacing notes

- **Track:** one continuous, driving-but-clean corporate/tech bed. "Curious/forward-momentum"
  vibe — not dramatic-trailer. Keep it under the VO (-18 to -22 LUFS under voice).
- **Hook (0:00–0:15):** start music on the `$965B` card hit; light whoosh/impact on each text
  card reveal.
- **Beat transitions:** small riser or soft cut on each beat boundary. Avoid hard EDM drops —
  this is a credibility explainer.
- **Beat 2 → 3 (the comparison + revenue):** let the music open up slightly; this is the
  "wow number" stretch.
- **CTA / Outro (7:20+):** drop music energy a touch, then a final button-click SFX on the
  subscribe animation and a clean end-card swell.
- **Pacing:** cut on the beat boundaries above; never sit on a single static text card longer
  than ~6s without movement (push-in, parallax, or b-roll behind it).

`ffprobe`, re-fit the SRT to the true length, concatenate/trim the clips to match, lay the
voiceover as the audio track, and output the rough cut MP4 with the SRT alongside.
